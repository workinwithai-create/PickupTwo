# PickupTwo

Two-bar live pickup desk for bedroom producers and AI-music finishers.

Suno and loop DAWs hard-cut the verse into the hook. The chorus slams in cold. Session players write two live bars of pickup — walk-up, snare flam, brass lift, bass climb, piano roll — so the unfinished song finally has a doorway. Then the chorus starts like someone meant it.

Live samples only. FluidR3 acoustic grand, upright bass, nylon guitar, trumpet, violin, and the PreEight acoustic kit. Loaded from the public PreEight sample CDN. No oscillators. Audio never leaves the tab.

This morning’s earlier ship was DieTwice (false-ending desk so a chorus can die and come back). PickupTwo is the missing **approach** so a hook does not slam in from silence.

## Loop

1. Pick a groove (Amber Room, Porch Light, Fold Night).
2. Pick a pickup (walk-up, snare flam, brass lift, bass climb, piano roll, nylon run, violin lean, kick push, stop then in, full pickup).
3. Hear the verse loop without the doorway.
4. Hear the full pass: four bars of verse, two bars of pickup, two bars of chorus landing.
5. Copy the punch list and bounce a WAV of bars 5–6.
